1. Purpose and Scope

This report establishes the measurement uncertainty budget for endotoxin results obtained by the Kinetic Chromogenic Bacterial Endotoxins Test. It summarises the four evaluated components — instrument, analyst, reagent and micropipette — and states the combined and expanded uncertainty for reporting purposes. All raw data, intermediate calculations and component summaries are retained in Appendices 2–5; this report refers to those appendices and does not reproduce their full derivation.

The budget applies to the BET procedure for haemodialysis concentrated solution and equivalent matrices validated under the same method, within the validated working range.

3. Measurement Model

The measurand is the endotoxin concentration C (EU/mL) predicted from the measured onset time t (s) using the log–log linear regression established during the Initial Qualification Test (Lysate Verification):

log10 t = a + b · log10 C

The regression parameters (b = −0.226, a = 3.075, r = −0.999, N = 20) and the derivation of the interpolation uncertainty are documented in Appendix 5. The BET procedure modelled comprises sample dilution (183 µL into 3.500 mL), the final well (100 µL sample + 100 µL reagent), the PPC spike (10 µL) and the standard-curve serial dilutions; the corresponding volumetric calculations are in Appendix 4.

Four independent components were quantified. Each component is a relative standard uncertainty, urel, evaluated in the cited appendix.

5. Combined Standard Uncertainty

The four components are independent and are combined by root-sum-square of the relative standard uncertainties:

uc,rel = √[ urel,instrument² + urel,analyst² + urel,reagent² + urel,micropipette² ]
uc,rel = √[(0.0497)² + (0.0872)² + (0.0999)² + (0.00195)²] = 0.1416

6. Expanded Uncertainty and Final Budget

The combined standard uncertainty is expanded using a coverage factor k = 2, corresponding to a level of confidence of approximately 95 % for a normal distribution:

Urel = k · uc,rel = 2 × 0.1416 = 0.2833 (28.3 %)

8. Reporting the Result

In accordance with ISO/IEC 17025:2017 clause 7.8.7.1, endotoxin results shall be reported with the expanded uncertainty. The following statement shall be appended to test reports:

Uncertainty statement. The reported endotoxin concentration C is associated with an expanded measurement uncertainty U = 28.3 % of the reported value, at a coverage factor k = 2, corresponding to a level of confidence of approximately 95 %. The uncertainty budget includes contributions from the microplate reader, analyst, reagent (lysate) calibration curve and micropipette operations, evaluated in accordance with ISO/IEC Guide 98-3 (GUM).

9. Assumptions, Limitations and Traceability

9.1 Assumptions

  • Component uncertainties are mutually independent; combination is therefore by root-sum-square.
  • Certificate expanded uncertainties were converted to standard uncertainties using k = 2.
  • The intercept bias of the microplate reader was treated as rectangular (divisor √3).
  • The reagent interpolation uncertainty was evaluated at 0.500 EU/mL with duplicate sample testing (m = 2), as detailed in Appendix 5.
  • Worst-case certificate values were applied for the 1 mL pipettes and for the 183 µL, 800 µL and 900 µL transfers, as detailed in Appendix 4.

9.2 Limitations

  • The budget applies to matrices validated under this method; extension to other products requires verification.
  • Re-evaluation is required on change of microplate reader, analyst cohort, lysate lot, pipette calibration status or the lysate verification regression model.
  • The reagent component is based on a single Initial Qualification Test dataset; periodic re-verification will improve its robustness.
  • The evaluation assumes the test is performed under validated method conditions. An out-of-specification PPC recovery invalidates the associated result independently of the stated uncertainty.

9.3 Traceability note

Full derivations, raw data and component summaries are held in Appendices 2–5. Where minor typographical or arithmetic inconsistencies appear in the source appendices, the summary values reported in each appendix have been adopted in this report; the detailed derivations remain in the respective appendix for traceability and correction at the next document revision.

10. Conclusion

A measurement uncertainty budget has been established for the Kinetic Chromogenic BET in accordance with ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and the GUM. The combined relative standard uncertainty is 0.1416, giving an expanded relative uncertainty of 28.3 % at k = 2 (approximately 95 % confidence). The value is fit for purpose for endotoxin limit testing, where results are compared against a limit concentration and supported by the PPC recovery criterion.

The dominant contributors are reagent interpolation and analyst variability; improvement actions should focus on these areas, for example increased replication in the lysate verification regression and periodic analyst proficiency monitoring. The micropipette contribution is negligible for this procedure.
