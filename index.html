1. Purpose and Scope

This report documents the measurement uncertainty (MU) budget for the standard curve and system repeatability, derived exclusively from the Initial Qualification Test (Lysate Verification) record. The measurand is the predicted endotoxin concentration \(C\) (in EU/mL) for a given onset time \(t\), based on the log-log linear regression model.

2. Method and Regression Parameters

The kinetic chromogenic method relates the onset time \(t\) to endotoxin concentration \(C\) through the empirical log–log relationship:

\[ \log_{10} t = a + b \cdot \log_{10} C \]

Note: In regression statistics, the within-run repeatability is inherently captured in the residual standard deviation (\(s_{y/x}\)), which is used to calculate \(u_{\text{cal}}\). Therefore, \(u_{\text{cal}}\) is the dominant and most appropriate component for this scope.

4. Quantification of Calibration Uncertainty

The uncertainty of a concentration predicted from the regression line is calculated using the standard formula for the uncertainty of an interpolated value:

\[ u_{\text{cal}}(\log_{10} C_0) = \frac{s_{y/x}}{|b|} \sqrt{ \frac{1}{m} + \frac{1}{N} + \frac{(x_0 - \bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}} } \]

where \(m\) is the number of replicate sample wells, \(N = 20\) the number of standard observations, and \(x_0 = \log_{10} C_0\). The relative standard uncertainty in the reported concentration is:

\[ u_{\text{cal,rel}}(C_0) = \ln(10) \cdot u_{\text{cal}}(\log_{10} C_0) \]

4.1 Worked example at Mid-Curve (\(C_0 = 0.500\ \mathrm{EU/mL}\))

For a sample measured in duplicate (\(m = 2\)):

\[ \begin{aligned} x_0 &= \log_{10}(0.500) = -0.30103 \\ x_0 - \bar{x} &= -0.30103 - (-0.2408) = -0.0602 \\ (x_0 - \bar{x})^2 &= 0.003625 \\ S_{xx} &= 35.47 \\ \\ u_{\text{cal}}(\log_{10}C) &= \left( \frac{0.0132}{0.226} \right) \cdot \sqrt{ \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{20} + \frac{0.003625}{35.47} } \\ &= 0.05841 \cdot \sqrt{ 0.500 + 0.050 + 0.000102 } \\ &= 0.05841 \cdot 0.7417 \\ &= 0.04333 \\ \\ u_{\text{cal,rel}}(C) &= 2.3026 \cdot 0.04333 \\ &= 0.0998 \approx 10.0\% \end{aligned} \]

6. Uncertainty Statement (Qualification Only)

Based strictly on the Initial Qualification Test, for a sample interpolated near the mid-point of the standard curve (0.5 EU/mL) using duplicate wells, the relative combined standard uncertainty is:

\[ u_{c,\text{rel}}(C) = 10.0\% \]

The expanded relative uncertainty at approximately 95 % confidence is:

\[ U_{\text{rel}} = k \cdot u_{c,\text{rel}} = 2 \times 10.0\% = 20.0\% \]

Important: This statement represents only the calibration and system repeatability uncertainty derived from the qualification data. To report a full ISO 17025 MU for a customer sample, this value must be combined (via root-sum-square) with uncertainties for sample dilution, product-matrix recovery, and intermediate precision.
