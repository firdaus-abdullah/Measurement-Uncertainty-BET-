1. Purpose and Scope

This report documents the measurement uncertainty (MU) budget for the quantitative determination of bacterial endotoxin by kinetic chromogenic LAL in aqueous samples, in accordance with ISO/IEC 17025:2017 clause 7.6 and the Eurachem/CITAC Guide (3rd ed., 2012).

The budget is expressed as relative standard uncertainty in the reported endotoxin concentration, \(u_{c,rel}(C)\), and expanded to \(U = k\cdot u_c\) with \(k = 2\) (≈95 % confidence). The measurand is:

C = endotoxin concentration in the sample, expressed in EU/mL (or EU/mg where applicable)

4. Measurement Model

The concentration of endotoxin in the sample is obtained from the measured onset time of the sample well(s):

log₁₀ C_sample = ( log₁₀ t_sample − a ) / b C_sample = 10 ^ [ ( log₁₀ t_sample − a ) / b ]

Once obtained, the concentration is corrected for dilution and (where applicable) expressed per unit mass or volume of product:

C_reported = C_sample · DF · ( 1 / V_or_m ) · R_recovery⁻¹

where \(DF\) is the total dilution factor, \(V\_or\_m\) the sample volume or mass basis, and \(R\) the method recovery (spike recovery in the product matrix).

6. Quantification of Individual Components

6.1 Standard-curve calibration, \(u_{cal}\)

The uncertainty of a concentration predicted from the regression line is calculated using the standard formula for the uncertainty of an interpolated value:

u_cal( log₁₀ C₀ ) = ( s_y/x / |b| ) · sqrt( 1/m + 1/N + ( x₀ − x̄ )² / S_xx )

where \(m\) is the number of replicate sample wells, \(N = 20\) the number of standard observations, \(x_0 = \log_{10} C_0\), and the other parameters are given in Section 3. The relative standard uncertainty in the reported concentration is:

u_cal,rel( C₀ ) = ln(10) · u_cal( log₁₀ C₀ )

Worked example at \(C_0 = 0.500\ \mathrm{EU/mL}\), \(m = 2\) (duplicate wells)

x₀ = log₁₀(0.500) = −0.30103 x₀ − x̄ = −0.30103 − (−0.2408) = −0.0602 (x₀ − x̄)² = 0.003625 S_xx = 35.47 u_cal(log₁₀C) = (0.0132 / 0.226) · sqrt( 1/2 + 1/20 + 0.003625/35.47 ) = 0.05841 · sqrt( 0.500 + 0.050 + 0.000102 ) = 0.05841 · 0.7417 = 0.04333 u_cal,rel(C) = 2.3026 · 0.04333 = 0.0998 ≈ 10.0 %

6.2 CSE potency / assignment, \(u_{CSE}\) ASSUMED

Convert the certificate expanded uncertainty to a standard uncertainty:

u_CSE = U_CSE / k_cert

Typical CSE certificates state \(U = 5\,\%\) at \(k = 2\), giving \(u_{CSE} = 2.5\,\%\). The actual value must be taken from the certificate of the CSE lot in use. For this draft budget, \(u_{CSE,rel} = 2.5\,\%\) is used as an illustrative placeholder.

6.3 Dilution and pipetting, \(u_{dil}\) ASSUMED

Dilution uncertainty is dominated by the pipettes/volumetric flasks used in the sample dilution chain. For a typical chain of three dilution steps (e.g., 1:10, 1:10, 1:10), and assuming a relative standard uncertainty of ≈2.5 % per step (from calibration certificate + temperature + operator), the combined dilution uncertainty is:

u_dil,rel = sqrt( 3 × 2.5² % ) ≈ 4.3 %

Replace with your own gravimetric / certificate-based value.

6.4 Sample repeatability, \(u_{rep}\) ASSUMED

For product samples, the within-run repeatability of the reported EU/mL (or EU/mg) is normally obtained from n ≥ 6 independent replicate determinations on the same sample:

u_rep,rel = RSD_replicates / sqrt( n )

For this draft, a product-sample RSD of 15 % (typical for BET in complex matrices) with n = 6 replicates is used, giving \(u_{rep,rel} = 15\,\% / \sqrt{6} \approx 6.1\,\%\). If the reported result is the mean of the replicates, use the standard uncertainty of the mean. For a single reported well, use the full RSD (15 %). This component must be replaced by your actual product data.

6.5 Product-matrix recovery, \(u_{rec}\) ASSUMED

Spike recovery is performed in the actual product matrix. If the mean recovery is \(R\) and the standard deviation of recoveries across \(n_{rec}\) independent spike determinations is \(s_R\):

u_rec,rel = s_R / ( R · sqrt( n_rec ) )

For this draft: assumed \(s_R = 20\,\%\), \(R = 100\,\%\), \(n_{rec} = 3\) ⇒ \(u_{rec,rel} \approx 11.5\,\%\). The actual recovery and its standard deviation must come from the product validation study.

6.6 Incubation temperature, \(u_{temp}\) ASSUMED

The report records 36.9–37.0 °C, i.e., a half-range of 0.05 °C around 36.95 °C. If the temperature sensitivity of the onset time is \(S_T\) (%/°C), then:

u_temp,rel = S_T · ( 0.05 / √3 )

For this draft, an illustrative \(S_T = 2\,\%/\text{°C}\) is used, giving \(u_{temp,rel} \approx 0.06\,\%\). A conservative placeholder of 1.0 % is used in the budget. The value should be confirmed from manufacturer data or an in-house temperature-challenge study.

6.7 Intermediate precision, \(u_{int}\) ASSUMED

Reproducibility contributions from different analysts, days and reagent lots (beyond within-run repeatability) are estimated from an intermediate-precision study:

u_int,rel = RSD_intermediate

A placeholder of 8.0 % is used in the draft budget. Replace with the RSD obtained from your intermediate-precision experiment.

11. Limitations and Required Actions

  1. The Lysate Verification report is not a product-specific validation report and cannot alone support a customer-facing MU statement.
  2. The standard-curve component \(u_{cal}\) is valid only within the calibrated range 0.0100–50.0 EU/mL. Results outside this range (including “< lowest standard”) must be reported qualitatively (e.g., “less than 0.0100 EU/mL”) and must not carry this MU statement.
  3. The budget is expressed for aqueous samples (water matrix). For product matrices (e.g., parenterals, biologicals), the recovery component must be re-derived from that product’s own spike-recovery data.
  4. The budget assumes duplicate sample wells. If single-well results are reported, use the m = 1 column of Section 6.1 (ucal ≈ 13.8 %), which increases the combined uncertainty accordingly.
  5. The CSE, dilution, recovery, sample-repeatability and intermediate-precision components are placeholders in this draft. The budget is complete in structure but not in traceability.
  6. The temperature sensitivity coefficient is assumed; a small temperature-challenge experiment (e.g., 36.5, 37.0, 37.5 °C) is recommended to confirm \(S_T\).
  7. Reassess the budget whenever a new CSE lot, lysate lot, reader, analyst or product matrix is introduced, and at least annually.
