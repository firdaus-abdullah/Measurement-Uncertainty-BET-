Uncertainty Analysis for Partial Volumes (400 µL & 900 µL)

The Problem: The calibration certificates only provide data for 100 µL, 500 µL, and 1000 µL. If your laboratory routinely dispenses 400 µL or 900 µL, using the uncertainty from the 1000 µL test point is technically incorrect (and often overly conservative).

To solve this, we use Linear Interpolation of the Relative Error (%) to estimate the bias at your specific target volumes. We will use the best-performing pipette (Certificate 3 - MSRP26023944) as our primary example, as its data is the most reliable.

1. Data Points from Certificate 3 (MSRP26023944)

Nominal Volume Systematic Error (Bias) Relative Error (%) Lab Expanded Uncertainty (U)
100 µL - 1.4975 µL - 1.50 % ± 0.14 µL
500 µL + 0.3918 µL + 0.08 % ± 0.31 µL
1000 µL + 2.8697 µL + 0.29 % ± 0.44 µL

2. Estimating Uncertainty for 400 µL

Since 400 µL falls between the 100 µL and 500 µL calibration points, we interpolate between those two data points.

Step A: Interpolate Relative Error

The interval is 400 µL (100 to 500). Your target (400 µL) is at the 75% mark of this interval.

Error at 400 µL = Error(100) + [ (400 - 100) / (500 - 100) ] × [ Error(500) - Error(100) ]
Error at 400 µL = -1.50% + [ 300 / 400 ] × [ 0.08% - (-1.50%) ]
Error at 400 µL = -1.50% + [ 0.75 ] × [ 1.58% ]
Error at 400 µL = -1.50% + 1.185% = -0.315 %

Step B: Calculate Estimated Absolute Bias (µL)

Estimated Bias = 400 µL × (-0.315 / 100) = -1.26 µL

Step C: Calculate Total Expanded Uncertainty

Note: We conservatively use the Lab U from the nearest larger test point (500 µL, U = ±0.31 µL, uc = 0.155 µL).

ubias = |-1.26| / √3 = 0.727 µL

utotal = √(uc² + ubias²) = √(0.155² + 0.727²) = 0.743 µL

Utotal = 2 × 0.743 = ± 1.49 µL

3. Estimating Uncertainty for 900 µL

Since 900 µL falls between the 500 µL and 1000 µL calibration points, we interpolate between those two data points.

Step A: Interpolate Relative Error

The interval is 400 µL (500 to 1000). Your target (900 µL) is at the 80% mark of this interval.

Error at 900 µL = Error(500) + [ (900 - 500) / (1000 - 500) ] × [ Error(1000) - Error(500) ]
Error at 900 µL = 0.08% + [ 400 / 500 ] × [ 0.29% - 0.08% ]
Error at 900 µL = 0.08% + [ 0.80 ] × [ 0.21% ]
Error at 900 µL = 0.08% + 0.168% = 0.248 %

Step B: Calculate Estimated Absolute Bias (µL)

Estimated Bias = 900 µL × (0.248 / 100) = +2.232 µL

Step C: Calculate Total Expanded Uncertainty

Note: We use the Lab U from the nearest test point (1000 µL, U = ±0.44 µL, uc = 0.22 µL).

ubias = |2.232| / √3 = 1.288 µL

utotal = √(uc² + ubias²) = √(0.22² + 1.288²) = 1.307 µL

Utotal = 2 × 1.307 = ± 2.61 µL

4. Important Warning: Cert 2 (MSRP26072890)

If you are using Certificate 2 (MSRP26072890) for your 900 µL testing, you have a much higher risk.
  • Its Relative Error at 500 µL is -0.38% and at 1000 µL is -0.60%.
  • Interpolated relative error at 900 µL is roughly -0.556%.
  • Estimated bias = -5.00 µL.
  • Total Expanded Uncertainty = ± 5.81 µL.
Because 900 µL is very close to the maximum volume of the pipette, any mechanical wear is magnified.

5. Expert Recommendation for Your SOP

Do not rely on interpolation for routine testing if you can avoid it.

  1. Request a custom calibration: When you send your pipettes out for calibration, specifically ask the vendor to test at 400 µL and 900 µL. Most calibration labs charge the same price but will adjust their test points to match your actual workflow.
  2. Perform internal verification: If you must use the certificate values, perform a quick gravimetric check in-house at 400 µL and 900 µL to confirm the interpolated values are accurate.
  3. Use the Interpolated Utotal values: For your uncertainty budgets, use ± 1.49 µL for 400 µL and ± 2.61 µL for 900 µL (based on Cert 3). This is far more accurate than using the 1000 µL limit of ± 3.34 µL for all your tests.
